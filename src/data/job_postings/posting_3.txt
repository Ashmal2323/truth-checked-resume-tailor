About the job
About Jeeny:

Jeeny is a mobile application that eases daily commuting and transportation. By connecting you with your preferred mode of transportation, we are fulfilling our aim of making mobility accessible, affordable, and flexible for all.

We are a joint venture between MEIG (Middle East Internet Group), Rocket Internet, and IMENA. Jeeny was established in 2014 as Easy Taxi. However, in 2016, it was revamped as Jeeny to cater to other services. Currently, we are operational in Saudi Arabia and Jordan.

We have offices in Riyadh, Jeddah, Madinah, Dammam, Khobar, Amman, Lahore, and Karachi.

About the Role:

We are looking for a highly skilled Senior DevOps Engineer to design, implement, and maintain scalable, secure, and highly available infrastructure. The ideal candidate will have strong experience in AWS cloud environments, CI/CD automation, monitoring, logging, database reliability, and distributed systems.

This role requires ownership mindset, deep troubleshooting capability, and collaboration with engineering, security, and data teams to ensure platform stability, performance, and cost efficiency.

Key Responsibilities:

Cloud & Infrastructure

Architect and manage highly available and scalable infrastructure on AWS
Design secure VPC architecture, IAM policies, networking, and security controls
Manage EC2, EKS (if applicable), RDS, S3, ELB/ALB, CloudFront, etc
Optimize cloud cost and implement FinOps best practices
Ensure disaster recovery and business continuity planning

CI/CD & Automation

Design and maintain CI/CD pipelines using Jenkins
Implement automated build, test, and deployment workflows
Integrate SonarQube for code quality gates
Implement Infrastructure as Code (Terraform/CloudFormation preferred)
Automate repetitive operational tasks

Monitoring, Logging & Observability

Manage ELK stack (Elasticsearch, Logstash, Kibana) for centralized logging
Implement log retention and optimization strategies
Configure and manage New Relic for APM and infrastructure monitoring
Set up alerting and incident response mechanisms
Improve system observability and SLO tracking

Database & Messaging Systems

Manage and optimize: MongoDB, MySQL, PostgreSQL, Redis, Kafka
Ensure database high availability, replication, and backup strategies
Perform performance tuning and troubleshooting
Monitor message queue performance and reliability

Security & Compliance

Implement DevSecOps practices
Ensure secure CI/CD pipelines
Perform infrastructure hardening
Work closely with security teams for vulnerability management
Ensure compliance with data protection standards

Reliability & Performance

Lead incident management and root cause analysis
Improve system reliability and uptime
Implement auto-scaling and performance optimization strategies
Define and monitor SLAs, SLOs, and KPIs

Requirements

Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or related field (or equivalent experience)
5+ years in DevOps / SRE / Cloud Engineering roles
Strong hands-on experience with AWS
Experience managing production systems at scale
Experience in high-traffic environments (ride-hailing / fintech / e-commerce preferred)
